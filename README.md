# first-respositiory

## Firefly Maze

An original maze-chase arcade game in a single file: `index.html`. Open it in any browser to play.

- Guide the firefly through the hedge maze and eat every glow seed.
- Four moths hunt you, each with its own strategy (direct chaser, ambusher, pincer, and a shy one).
- Eat a flare to make the moths flee, then catch them for 200 / 400 / 800 / 1600 points.
- Bonus items appear mid-level; extra life at 10,000 points; speed rises each night.

Controls: arrow keys or WASD, P to pause, M to mute. On phones, swipe or use the on-screen pad.

## Daily List

A simple to-do list in one file: `todo.html`. Type a task and press Add, tick the box when it's done, or click × to delete it. Your list is saved in your browser.

## Time Anchor

A visual timer for ADHD time blindness: `time-anchor.html`.

- A big dial drains as time passes and shifts from green to amber to red as the end gets close.
- Remaining time is shown in plain words and in things you can feel ("about 4 songs", "about one shower").
- Time it "for a while" (5/15/25/45 min or your own number) or "until a time" (e.g. when you need to leave).
- Optional gentle check-ins every 5, 10 or 15 minutes ask whether you're still on task.
- A clear "time's up" alert with a one-tap "5 more minutes". The browser tab title shows the countdown too.

### Time Anchor phone app

`time-anchor-app/` is an installable version of Time Anchor. It has a home-screen icon, opens full-screen and works offline.
Once GitHub Pages is on, open https://corey-shields.github.io/first-respositiory/time-anchor-app/ in Safari on iPhone, tap Share, then tap **Add to Home Screen**.
